# Spike record

Use one record in the active plan or resolved research destination. Link the
source work item instead of maintaining a second story-status record.

```markdown
## Spike: <single question>

### Contract

- Decision affected: <source link and why evidence is needed>.
- Hypotheses: <testable alternatives>.
- Experiment and bounds: <minimal check, attempts, and any user timebox>.
- Success/rejection/inconclusive criteria: <observable conditions>.
- Ownership: <scratch/output paths and production exclusions>.
- Preservation: <current dirty baseline, exact backups, and cleanup approach>.

### Evidence and outcome

- Commands/environment: <actual commands, versions, and outcomes>.
- Finding: supported / rejected / inconclusive — <evidence and limits>.
- Confidence: <reasoned confidence and what would change it>.
- Remaining unknowns: <specific gaps>.
- Next action: <supported path or next question; no forced answer>.
- Production implications: <work requiring caller scope/authorization>.
- Cleanup/recovery: <restored owned changes or preserved residue and paths>.
```

Do not rewrite the original experiment criteria to match the result. Record a
changed hypothesis or follow-up explicitly with its reason and remaining budget.
